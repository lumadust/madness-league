Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026.

This policy describes how data is processed in the Madness League app and its related services. The data controller is Simone Sapienza, an individual who develops the game under the name LumaDust. LumaDust is an unregistered name and does not refer to a company. Privacy contact: lumadust.support@gmail.com.

1. Data processed and purposes

  • Profile and progress: player name, experience, virtual currency, inventory, unlocked content, rewards, preferences and history of in-game operations. This data is used to maintain your profile, grant rewards and restore progress.
  • Authentication identifiers: Unity Authentication creates a player identifier, including when you sign in anonymously. When Google Play Games linking is available and you choose to use it, the technical data needed to authenticate and link the accounts is processed.
  • Cloud saves: Unity Cloud Save may store a copy of your profile associated with your player identifier for backup and synchronization.
  • Purchases: product and transaction identifiers, receipts or purchase tokens, and entitlement status may be processed to verify purchases, grant content and prevent duplication or fraud. Payments are handled by Google Play; the app does not request payment card numbers.
  • Advertising: Google AdMob and the UMP consent platform may process your IP address, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic data for ad delivery, measurement, consent management and fraud prevention, depending on the configuration and applicable choices.
  • Technical services: providers may process information about your device, system, app version, connection and service operation. Unity Remote Config distributes game configurations and Cloud Code handles backend operations.
  • Support: if you contact us, we process your email address and the information you choose to provide so that we can respond to your request. Do not send passwords, payment card numbers or other secrets.

The custom telemetry implemented in the code runs locally in development builds and is disabled in non-Development builds, including the InternalTesting profile. This does not disable technical processing performed by third-party SDKs. InternalTesting builds use the UGS development environment and test AdMob IDs.

2. Providers and data disclosure

Necessary data may be disclosed to providers handling authentication, cloud services, configurations, purchase verification, advertising and consent, and to authorities when required by law.

  • Unity: Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.
  • Google: privacy for Google Play, Play Games and Google services.
  • Google: advertising technologies and data processed by the Mobile Ads SDK.

Processing may take place in countries other than your own, including outside the European Economic Area. Transfers subject to the GDPR must rely on a mechanism provided for by law, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses, where applicable. You can contact us for information about the safeguards relating to your data. Providers’ policies also describe their processing and transfers.

3. Legal bases and choices

Where the GDPR applies, we process the data needed to provide the game, manage your profile, synchronize progress, grant purchases and respond to support requests to perform the service you request. Advertising processing that requires consent is based on consent. Data needed to comply with legal obligations is processed on the basis of those obligations. Service security and fraud prevention may rely on our legitimate interest in protecting users and the service, provided that the rights and freedoms of the individuals concerned do not override that interest. The specific conditions described below apply to minors.

When required, the app displays the advertising consent form. Settings show “Privacy options” when the Google service requires this option to be available. Preferences can also be managed through your device’s privacy and advertising settings.

4. Retention and deletion

Local data is stored on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data does not automatically delete cloud saves, provider-held data or purchase records.

To request deletion of your profile and associated data, email lumadust.support@gmail.com with the subject “Madness League — Data deletion”. Include your player name and, if available, your profile identifier. You can submit a request even after uninstalling the app. Proportionate information may be needed to identify the profile and verify that the request comes from its owner. Do not send credentials. Identifying an anonymous account may be difficult if useful information is no longer available.

The request covers your cloud profile, progress, preferences and other associated data under our control. After the request has been verified, data for which there is no legitimate reason for retention is deleted or anonymized. Deletion may result in the permanent loss of progress and associated content. It does not delete your Google account and does not constitute a refund request to Google Play.

We retain profile data to allow use and recovery of the service, until the profile is deleted or the data is no longer needed for those purposes. Support communications are retained to handle the request and any disputes. Limited purchase and security records may be retained where necessary for legal obligations, fraud prevention or the protection of legal rights. Any residual backup copies are subject to providers’ retention cycles. On request, we will explain the categories retained, the reasons, and the periods or criteria applicable to the specific case.

We respond without undue delay and within the time limits set by applicable law; for requests subject to the GDPR, generally within one month, notifying you of any permitted extensions and their reasons. If a request cannot be fulfilled, we explain why and the remedies available. Data processed independently by providers is also subject to their policies and procedures for exercising your rights.

5. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. You may lodge a complaint with the competent authority, such as the Italian Data Protection Authority in Italy.

6. Minors

Protecting minors’ data requires specific safeguards. Children’s access to the game is conditional on the availability of an age-appropriate mode and compliance with the requirements of the country of use. The current version does not yet provide such a mode: children must not use the app’s online services until it is introduced. This policy does not authorize the use of services that are not appropriate for a user’s age.

Before opening the service to a mixed-age audience, neutral age screening, data minimization, restrictions on identifiers and online features and, where required, verifiable parental or guardian consent will be necessary. Children and users whose age is unknown must not be shown personalized ads or be subject to advertising processing incompatible with applicable law and Google Play Families policies.

A parent or guardian can contact us at lumadust.support@gmail.com to request information, access, rectification or deletion of a minor’s data. We will ask only for the information necessary to verify the request. If we learn that we have processed a minor’s data without a valid legal basis, we will take the necessary steps to stop the processing and delete the data, subject to legal obligations.

7. Security and updates

The project uses authentication services and protected cloud communications. No system provides absolute security. This policy will be updated when processing practices change; the published page will display the update date and any necessary notices.
