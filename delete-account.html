Account and data deletion

Last updated: November 3, 2026

You can request deletion of your Madness League account and associated data by contacting Simone Sapienza, the game’s developer under the name LumaDust. Requests are free of charge and can be submitted even after uninstalling the app.

How to submit a request

  1. Email lumadust.support@gmail.com with the subject Madness League account deletion.
  2. Include your player name and, if you know it, your profile identifier. State that you want to delete your account and all associated data. You do not need to reinstall the game to submit a request.
  3. We will reply to identify your profile and verify that the request comes from its owner. Your player name alone may not be sufficient: we will ask only for the information necessary for verification. For anonymous profiles without recoverable identifiers, verification may not be possible; if so, we will explain why.

Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or full payment card details.

Request deletion by email

If the button does not open your email app, copy the address and send the message using your preferred email service.

What data is deleted

Once verified, the request covers your game account and associated personal data under our control, including data handled on our behalf by service providers:

  • Your Madness League account identifier and authentication links.
  • Your profile and cloud saves: player name, progress, experience, statistics, virtual currency, inventory, unlocked content and preferences.
  • Other personal data associated with the profile, subject to the retention exceptions described below.

This operation may permanently remove progress and content associated with your profile. It does not delete your Google or Google Play Games account and does not constitute a request for a purchase refund.

Time frames and data that may be retained

We handle requests without undue delay. For requests subject to the GDPR, we generally respond within one month of receipt; any permitted extension will be communicated within that period, together with the reasons. If information is needed to verify the owner, we will explain the necessary steps.

We do not apply a blanket additional retention period to the active profile after deletion. Only information necessary for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention or handling disputes may be retained, such as transaction records and records of the support request. If an exception applies, our response will explain what data is retained, why, and the relevant retention period or criterion. We do not claim that all user data is automatically deleted within 90 days.

Any residual copies in providers’ backups follow their retention and deletion cycles; applicable time frames will be clarified when handling the request. Processing carried out independently by Google, including Google account data and Google Play records, is also subject to Google’s privacy procedures.

Data on your device

This procedure concerns your account and data held by services. To remove local saves as well, clear the app’s data in Android settings or uninstall the game. Uninstalling alone does not delete cloud data. Avoid using the game while your request is being handled: subsequent use may create a new profile.
